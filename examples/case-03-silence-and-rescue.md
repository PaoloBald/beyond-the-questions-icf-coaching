# Case 03: The silence I couldn't stand

*Fictional case written for illustration. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.*

**Focus:** filling silence after an emotional statement. **Competencies touched:** CC5 Maintains Presence, CC4 Cultivates Trust and Safety, CC7 Evokes Awareness.
**Distinctions offered:** Body–Emotions–Language; emotion vs. mood.

## Excerpt shared by the coach

> **Cl:** …and then I realized I've been in this job for eleven years just because I was afraid. *(pause)*
> **C:** That's a big realization. And you know, a lot of people stay for security, it's very human. What would you like to do with this insight?
> **Cl:** I don't know. Maybe update my CV?

## Phase 1: Moments

**AI:** Two moments, without evaluation:
1. The pause after "because I was afraid", and how long it lasted. (CC5)
2. "A lot of people stay for security, it's very human" → "What would you like to do with this insight?" (CC4, CC7, CC8)

Where would you like to start?
**Coach:** The pause. I think it was about one second.

## Phase 2: Reflective dialogue (abridged)

**AI:** What was happening in your body in that second?
**Coach:** My chest tightened. I leaned forward.

**AI:** And what emotion was there?
**Coach:** Something like… worry. That she'd feel bad. And maybe embarrassment, as if I'd caused it.

**AI:** What did that emotion make you want to do?
**Coach:** Make it OK. Reassure her. Move on.

**AI:** A distinction, offered tentatively. Ontological coaching sees body, emotion and language as linked. A tight chest and worry predispose us to certain actions, here reassuring and moving on, and not to others, like staying silent or exploring the fear. One possible reading is that your words ("it's very human") came from your body's discomfort more than from what she needed. How does this land?
**Coach:** Yes. I normalized it to calm *myself*.

**AI:** What might have been possible if you had stayed with the silence?
**Coach:** She might have said more about the fear. Where it comes from, what it's protecting. The CV came too soon.

**AI:** What would help you stay longer next time?
**Coach:** Breathing out. Leaning back instead of forward. Maybe just repeating her word: "afraid".

## Phase 3: Integration

**AI:** What do you see now that you didn't see before?
**Coach:** That my body moves before my mind. Discomfort turns into reassurance.
**AI:** What will you try?
**Coach:** After a strong statement, one slow breath out and lean back before speaking. I'll notice if I'm leaning forward.
